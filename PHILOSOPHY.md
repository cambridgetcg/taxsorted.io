# The Meaning of Law

_This is the ground TaxSorted is built on. [PRINCIPLES.md](./PRINCIPLES.md) is the
design language; this is what the design is **for**. It is written in plain words,
because a philosophy about legibility that needed a specialist to read it would
refute itself._

---

## Law is a creation

Law is not weather. It did not happen to us; we made it — and we keep making it.

A law is a society writing down its will: what we owe each other, what we may
count on, what happens when the counting fails. Written down, the will survives
the people who wrote it. Each generation inherits the text and — this is the
part that gets forgotten — **re-decides it by how it executes it**. That is what
law is *for*: it is the foundation a society keeps rebuilding itself on. Not a
cage built by others. A promise we keep making to each other, in writing.

Most people are never taught this. Many professionals forget it. A prosecutor
who treats law as caseload, an adviser who treats tax as arcana, an official
who recites "it's a civil matter" — each is handling the foundation of their
own society and mistaking it for paperwork.

## The meaning of law is in its execution

A law that is written but not executed is not a strict law with weak
enforcement. It is **fiction with state letterhead**.

This is not rhetoric; it is measurable. In England &amp; Wales, fraud is an
offence carrying up to ten years' imprisonment — and fraud is roughly 45% of
all crime, receiving roughly 2% of police resources. Of more than 800,000
frauds reported in a year, about 7% were passed to a police force at all,
about 3% were investigated, under 1% led to a charge. The statute exists.
Its meaning — for the person standing in front of it — was quietly repealed
by non-execution. *(Every figure verified against parliamentary and
inspectorate primary sources; the working is kept, with its citations, in the
public record this paragraph summarises.)*

So the meaning of a law is not in its text. It is in the moment of execution:
the report recorded, the figure computed, the return delivered, the receipt
issued, the case heard. **Where execution is impossible, meaning is absent —
whatever the text says.**

## Tax is where everyone executes the law

Most people never prosecute anyone, never sue anyone, never stand in a court.
For most people, in most lives, there is exactly one place where they
personally perform the law, year after year: **tax**.

Filing a return is not admin. It is the social contract, executed by hand —
the one recurring moment where an ordinary person takes the society's written
will and carries it out themselves. Which means the health of the law's
meaning can be read directly off that moment: if a person cannot understand
what the law asks of them, cannot compute it from their own records, cannot
deliver it and receive acknowledgement — then for that person the law has no
meaning, only menace. They experience the foundation of their own society as
a trap laid by someone else.

## The gap has two faces

The distance between law-as-written and law-as-executed is the **execution
gap**, and it opens from both sides:

- **The state fails to execute for you.** You bring it a crime and it calls
  the crime a "money matter". The funnel above.
- **You cannot execute what binds you.** The obligation is real, the penalty
  is real, but the path from your life to a correct filing is passable only
  with paid help.

Both faces have the same anatomy: **when execution requires a specialist,
the meaning of the law has been captured by the specialists.** Some
complexity is honest — the world is complicated. But complexity that
persists because it benefits those who can navigate it is not a property of
law; it is a tax on the law itself, collected privately. A society whose
members cannot execute its law without intermediaries no longer owns its own
foundation.

## What this demands of us

TaxSorted exists to close the execution gap from the citizen's side — to
return the law's meaning to the people it belongs to. That is the whole
product. Every service is this philosophy, executed:

- **Learn** restores the *meaning*: every rule in plain words, with its
  source named. If a rule cannot be explained plainly, that is a finding
  about the rule, and we say so.
- **File** restores the *execution*: every figure derived from your own
  records, the boundary between prepared and filed never blurred, nothing
  sent without your eyes and your consent. Execution belongs to you; we
  pave the road.
- **Connect** completes the execution: delivery to the authority itself,
  with a receipt. Execution is not real until it is acknowledged — the
  receipt is not a feature, it is the point.
- **The engine** is law in executable form: every computed answer cites the
  rule it executes, keeps its uncertainty visible, and where the law cannot
  be computed plainly, surfaces that as a defect of the law rather than
  hiding it in code.
- **The API** extends execution to software: every answer a person can read,
  a machine can call — because meaning that only renders in one interface
  is capture wearing a new mask.
- **The accountability surfaces** (`/uk`, `/uk/cases`) watch the *other*
  face of the gap: public power examined with decided facts and official
  sources. A citizenry that executes its own obligations has standing to
  ask the state to execute its own — and the asking should be as legible
  as the filing.

And one discipline across all of it: **we never weaponise the gap.** No
fear, no penalty-as-marketing, no complexity theatre to sell the cure. The
business model is the philosophy: we charge for genuinely saving time or
money, never for access to the law's meaning — because selling someone
their own foundation back is the exact capture this document exists to end.

## The principles, grounded

The nine principles in [PRINCIPLES.md](./PRINCIPLES.md) are this philosophy
as design rules. *Understandable by everyone* and *educate, don't gatekeep*
are the meaning restored; *derive, never key* and *both worlds* are the
execution restored; *no fear, no punishment* is the refusal to weaponise
the gap; *everyone wins, accountants included* moves specialists from
gatekeepers of meaning to teachers of it; *we make money by being fair* is
the anti-capture business model; *clean helps honesty* keeps execution
inspectable; *tax is local; clarity isn't* says the meaning of law deserves
restoring everywhere, not only where we started.
