# People-power tax framework

_Product doctrine for helping a person or business understand tax, reduce its lawful burden,
act with consent and keep the proof._

This doctrine sits beneath individual tax modules and beside
[TaxSorted as accounting software](ACCOUNTING-PRODUCT.md). It does not promise that every tax,
entity or jurisdiction is covered today. It says how every covered path must work.

## The promise

TaxSorted helps people pay the right tax — no more and no less — with the least avoidable cost,
work, uncertainty and loss of control.

The law remains visible. The person keeps the choice. The product does the repeatable work,
names what it cannot know, sends nothing without consent, keeps the receipt and makes correction
or challenge as legible as compliance.

Returning power does not mean finding the lowest tax number at any cost. It means that a person
can see the rule, discover an intended entitlement, compare real choices, act from their own
records, reproduce the answer, correct a mistake, challenge a decision and leave with their work.

## Total lawful burden

TaxSorted minimises the **total lawful burden**, not tax alone:

```text
tax due
+ cash-flow and timing cost
+ direct fees and transaction costs
+ record-keeping and filing work
+ uncertainty, error and challenge risk
+ loss of liquidity, flexibility or control
+ material non-tax consequences
```

These parts stay separate. A smaller tax bill can be a worse outcome when it costs more, locks
money away, changes legal rights, creates risk or defeats the person's actual purpose. There is no
single mystery score and no automatic meaning of “best”.

## The six powers

Every complete journey returns six powers:

1. **Know** — map the taxpayer, role, entity, jurisdiction, period, facts, evidence and unknowns.
2. **See** — show duties, rights, reliefs, choices, deadlines, sources and product boundaries.
3. **Choose** — compare like-for-like scenarios across the whole lawful burden; the person chooses.
4. **Do** — prepare and carry out only the action the person deliberately approved.
5. **Prove** — preserve evidence, derivation, rule version, submission state and authority receipt.
6. **Challenge** — make correction, review, appeal, complaint and payment-difficulty routes visible.

The short loop is:

```text
Know -> See -> Choose -> Do -> Prove -> Challenge
```

No module has returned power if it stops at a calculation and leaves the person unable to act,
prove or put the answer right.

## Two spines, kept separate

Planning concerns a possible future. Compliance concerns what actually happened.

```text
Planning:   possible event -> position -> lawful options -> comparison -> human choice -> action
Compliance: actual evidence -> event -> books -> accounts -> tax -> approval -> submission -> receipt
```

A scenario is never a record. A forecast never silently becomes a return figure. Once a person
acts, the resulting evidence enters the compliance spine in the ordinary way. The two spines may
meet at the person's position and chosen action; they must not share unlabelled facts or money.

## The contract of every tax module

Each module answers the same questions, in this order:

1. What changed or may change?
2. Which person, entity, activity, jurisdiction and date does it concern?
3. Which facts change the answer, and which remain unknown?
4. What does the law require?
5. Which rights, allowances, reliefs, elections and help routes may apply?
6. Which lawful choices still exist before action?
7. What does each scenario change: tax, cash, cost, work, risk, flexibility and other rights?
8. What is automatic, what needs a claim or election, and what is the deadline?
9. What evidence must exist and be kept?
10. Where does the product stop or require review?
11. How can the result be corrected, challenged or handed to a professional?
12. Which rules, sources, dates and engine version produced the answer?

Every result distinguishes `unknown`, `not provided`, `not applicable`, `zero` and `no`. None may
stand in for another. Every source says what it supports and what it does not prove.

## Capability states

A module claims only the powers it has actually earned:

```text
mapped -> explained -> classified -> calculated -> compared -> prepared
       -> approved -> submitted -> received -> accepted/rejected
       -> settled -> corrected/challenged
```

- **Mapped** means the path and authority are known.
- **Explained** means the rule is stated in plain words with a source and effective period.
- **Classified** means supplied facts have been placed under a rule without inventing missing facts.
- **Calculated** means a bounded amount is reproducible from those facts and that rule version.
- **Compared** means genuinely like-for-like scenarios show all material burden dimensions.
- **Prepared** means an action or filing is ready for review, not authorised.
- **Approved** records deliberate consent to the exact action and payload.
- **Submitted** means the exact approved payload reached the submission service with conclusive
  transport proof. A button press, pre-send failure or delivery-unknown attempt is not submitted.
- **Delivery unknown** is a visible branch when an attempt has no conclusive transport proof; it
  must not be relabelled as submitted or retried blindly.
- **Received** means an authority acknowledgement is tied to the exact payload; its meaning still
  needs to be read.
- **Accepted** and **rejected** record the authority's substantive outcome without destroying the
  payload, response or reason.
- **Settled** means payment, repayment or closure of the balance is evidenced. Knowing the balance
  alone is not settlement.
- **Corrected/challenged** means the route and its evidence remain connected to the original result.

`needs_review`, `outside_scope` and `source_overdue` are complete, honest outcomes. “Filed” must
never blur preparation, approval, transmission, acknowledgement and settlement.

Capability and fact time are separate axes. A reproducible full-year scenario may be
**calculated**, but its visible fact-period state remains **projection**. It is never described as
completed, actual or return-ready. A completed-year state is available only after that tax year
has ended and its records are complete; year-to-date facts do not inherit either label.

Putting two bounded calculations side by side does not by itself earn the **compared** state. A
module may ship as **calculated** when it states every unassessed burden dimension, makes no
recommendation and never labels the smaller intermediate number “best” or “saved”. It earns
**compared** only when the material whole-burden consequences are genuinely like for like.

## Lawful minimisation taxonomy

TaxSorted opens lawful routes in increasing order of judgement and risk:

1. **Get the facts and arithmetic right.** Prevent duplicated income, missed costs, wrong periods,
   stale rates and other overpayment or underpayment errors.
2. **Use intended entitlements.** Surface allowances, deductions, credits, reliefs, losses,
   exemptions and repayment rights for their stated purpose.
3. **Protect timing and cash flow.** Explain deadlines, elections, instalments, deferrals and
   official payment-help routes without disguising the eventual liability.
4. **Compare real choices before the event.** Model genuine timing, legal form, remuneration,
   ownership or wrapper choices with their full non-tax effects visible.
5. **Stop for specialist judgement.** Residence and treaties, trusts, reorganisations,
   insolvency, investigations, pension transfers, complex property or VAT, employment status and
   other materially uncertain paths require an appropriately competent reviewer.

TaxSorted does not create, encourage or optimise artificial or contrived arrangements. It never
helps hide income, fabricate evidence, backdate an action, misstate residence, relabel a fact or
pretend control was given up when it was not. A marketed scheme is not safe because HMRC has not
named it, and registration with HMRC is not proof that an adviser is qualified or competent.

## Consent, advice and data boundaries

- Explain what a rule does; do not quietly decide what a person should value.
- State “under these inputs, this rule produces this result,” not “you should do this.”
- Let the person select scenarios. Do not preselect a consequential tax choice.
- A threshold gap may be calculated. Turning it into a pension, investment, company-structure or
  disposal recommendation requires separate suitability and regulatory consideration.
- Do not recommend a particular security, pension product, provider, lender or adviser unless the
  product has the authority and controls required for that activity.
- Customer-specific automated tax help, paid help, acting for another person and interaction with
  an authority can create different registration, supervision and professional duties. Each new
  commercial or representative module needs a recorded boundary review before release.
- Permission to read records is not permission to edit them. Permission to prepare is not
  permission to file. Filing authority is not payment authority or authority to represent someone
  in a dispute. Each grant is separate, narrow, visible, revocable and logged.
- Collect only facts that change a covered answer. Keep personal, household and legal entities
  separate unless a named rule requires the relationship.
- Learning and comparison should work without an account where practical. Export and deletion are
  ordinary product paths, not support requests.
- A generic language model may explain a bounded, sourced result; it may not invent a rule,
  override the deterministic engine or fill an unknown fact.

## No-dark-pattern rule

TaxSorted never turns fear, confusion or tax savings into pressure.

- No fake urgency. A real deadline names its authority, period and actual consequence.
- No shaming, penalty theatre, countdown theatre or implication of HMRC endorsement.
- No visually hidden decline, defer, undo, export, delete, revoke or leave path.
- No claim that “you saved £X” without the baseline, assumptions, costs, uncertainty and realised
  outcome needed to make that statement true.
- No ranking solely by estimated tax saved, provider commission or TaxSorted revenue.
- No fee, reward or interface prominence that grows merely because a displayed refund or saving
  grows. Contingent refund or savings fees do not belong in self-service decision paths.
- No gamification of aggressive tax outcomes. Play may reward understanding, complete evidence and
  honest correction; actual planning, approval and filing remain calm.
- Tests optimise for comprehension, correctness, informed refusal and successful exit — not time
  on site, conversion or the amount of tax displaced.

## Jurisdiction, time and source truth

Every calculation and comparison binds itself to the relevant taxpayer or entity, role, activity,
jurisdiction, event date, tax year or accounting period, residence assumptions and rule version.

Legislation, case law, authority guidance, announced policy and TaxSorted interpretation keep
their different meanings. Announced or draft rules never masquerade as law in force. The system
records both when a rule legally applies and when TaxSorted learned or last verified it. Historical
answers remain reproducible after a rule changes. A review-overdue rule stops calculation or
falls back to a plainly labelled explanation; it does not carry on silently.

## Coverage map

Individual journeys are organised around life rather than tax-form names:

- work, PAYE and benefits;
- self-employment and property income;
- family, childcare and caring;
- home and property transactions;
- saving, investing and pensions;
- moving country or working across borders;
- gifts, inheritance, trusts and estates;
- inability to pay, penalties, errors and disputes.

Business journeys follow the life of the undertaking:

- start and choose a legal form;
- sell, buy and consider registration duties;
- spend, invest and use intended reliefs;
- employ people and run payroll;
- pay owners and directors;
- raise finance, grow and trade across borders;
- restructure, sell, succeed or close;
- correct errors, answer enquiries, dispute or manage cash difficulty.

One person may be an employee, sole trader, landlord, director and trustee at once. A company is
not its owner. Separate taxpayers, activities, books, permissions and filings stay separate,
joined only by explicit roles and relationships required by a rule.

## Release tests

A module does not ship until all applicable tests answer **yes**:

1. **Need:** Does it solve a named user event or decision rather than expose an internal tax form?
2. **Authority:** Is every load-bearing rule tied to its source kind, force, effective period,
   retrieval date and review date?
3. **Facts:** Are missing facts visible, contradictions blocked and zero always explicit?
4. **Scope:** Are jurisdictions, periods, entities, supported cases and exclusions clear before
   the result?
5. **Reproduction:** Can the same facts and version reproduce every material figure and reason?
6. **Comparison:** Are scenarios like-for-like, separate from actual records and complete across
   the total lawful burden?
7. **Advice boundary:** Has the module been checked for tax-adviser, anti-money-laundering,
   financial-services, legal and representative-activity boundaries appropriate to its function
   and business model?
8. **Consent:** Can the person inspect the exact action and payload, decline it without penalty,
   and later see who did what and when?
9. **Failure:** Do stale sources, outages, rejection and partial completion fail visibly without
   inventing success or losing prior evidence?
10. **Proof:** Are preparation, approval, submission, receipt, settlement and correction distinct
    and connected?
11. **Recourse:** Does every duty, bill or penalty expose the relevant correction, help and
    challenge route?
12. **Control:** Can the person export, revoke, delete and leave without forfeiting access to the
    public law or their own understandable record?
13. **Fairness:** Is the interface free of pressure, biased ranking, hidden cost and revenue-led
    steering, including for people using assistive technology or another supported language?
14. **Machine parity:** Does each safely exposed human answer have the same scope, uncertainty,
    source trail and state distinctions in its machine form?

Passing a test once does not make it permanently true. Fiscal events, new law, court decisions,
authority guidance, source review dates, product behaviour and commercial terms can reopen it.

## Official foundation

These official sources were last reviewed for this doctrine on 25 August 2026:

- [HMRC Charter](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/hmrc-charter/the-hmrc-charter) —
  clear information, help claiming entitlements, lower compliance cost, fair treatment and rights
  to review and appeal.
- [HMRC's distinction between planning and avoidance](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/avoidance-handling-process/ahp1300)
  — intended use of reliefs is different from artificial or contrived arrangements seeking an
  unintended advantage.
- [HMRC Standard for Agents](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/hmrc-the-standard-for-agents/the-hmrc-standard-for-agents)
  — realistic facts, credible law, visible uncertainty, clear communication and no arrangements
  contrary to clear Parliamentary intent.
- [Reasonable care](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/reasonable-care-tax-returns-and-other-documents)
  — records and complete, accurate facts remain necessary even when another person helps.
- [Tax appeals and statutory review](https://www.gov.uk/tax-appeals/review-of-a-tax-or-penalty-decision)
  — personal recourse is part of the tax journey, not a separate afterthought.
- [Mandatory adviser registration](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-and-when-you-need-to-register-as-a-tax-adviser-with-hmrc)
  and [anti-money-laundering supervision](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/money-laundering-regulations-accountancy-service-provider-registration)
  — software, personalised help, commercial activity and representation need function-specific
  boundary review; a disclaimer is not the boundary.
- [Office of Tax Simplification review](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ots-review-of-tax-simplification/review-of-simplification-approach-and-interpretation)
  — technology should carry administrative work without taking informed choice away from people.

## Boundary record: first trading-allowance calculation

Reviewed 25 August 2026; source review due 25 November 2026.

- **Function released:** a free, self-service arithmetic tool for exactly one sole trade. The
  visitor supplies two money figures and one fact-period state governing every answer, confirms
  that this is their only relevant trade or miscellaneous-income source, confirms the
  relevant-income measurement and basis-period transition-profit boundaries, checks the
  Rent-a-Room boundary and supplies the excluded-payment fact for relevant trading or
  miscellaneous income. Inputs remain in page memory unless the visitor deliberately downloads a
  receipt.
- **Capability claimed:** `calculated`, with the whole-burden `compared` state explicitly
  incomplete. During the open 2026–27 year, a successful result is visibly a calculated
  full-year projection, not an actual or return figure. It calculates trading profit only; it
  does not calculate final tax, determine suitability, recommend a product or structure, make an
  election, prepare a return or act for the visitor.
- **Safety boundary:** another relevant trade or miscellaneous-income source, a possible trade
  loss, year-to-date facts, a prematurely claimed completed year, an incomplete income measure,
  adjustment income, post-cessation receipts, a basis-period transition-profit amount,
  Rent-a-Room receipts or uncertainty, unresolved excluded-payment facts, malformed dates and
  overdue sources stop the calculation. Property-profit calculations, property-allowance payer
  tests and partnership income are outside it.
- **Action boundary:** where an election is needed, 31 January 2029 is recorded as the normal
  on-time limit for 2026–27. A late return can sometimes carry an election within the general
  four-year claim limit; this module flags that review route rather than deciding that it is open.
- **Current functional classification:** user-operated tax calculation software with no fee,
  confidential intake, saved case, live dialogue, representative action or authority submission.
  This recorded scope is why the release does not present itself as a tax adviser service. It is
  not a permanent legal exemption or a substitute for case-specific regulatory advice.
- **Reopen the review before:** charging, accepting private case files, saving or monitoring a
  person’s position, adding customer-specific dialogue, recommending suitability-dependent
  action, making an election, filing, paying, corresponding with HMRC or representing a person.
  Recheck tax-adviser registration, anti-money-laundering supervision, privacy, professional
  competence and any financial-services or legal boundary then.

## The lasting measure

TaxSorted succeeds when the person can say, in their own words:

> I know what applies, what remains unknown, what choices are truly mine, what I approved, what
> happened, how I can prove it, and what I can do if it is wrong.

That is tax understood, executed and answerable to the people it affects.
