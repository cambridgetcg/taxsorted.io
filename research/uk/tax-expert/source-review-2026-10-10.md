## Open questions for a qualified tax adviser

These were not settled by this review. Where the engine's wording overreached, it was narrowed; the underlying question is still open. Each item names the engine's current behaviour, which stays in place until a qualified decision is recorded.

1. **Reach of an exclusion notice** (`uksi-2026-336`). Reg 18(2)(b)/(3) reaches obligations for the digital-obligation tax year containing any in-place day, and obligations treated as ceasing. Should an HMRC-approved exclusion be checked against the notice's in-place dates (reg 19(5), (7)) before the classifier returns exempt for 2026/27? *Now:* `hmrc-approved` returns exempt with no date input; the headline says the dates are not checked.
2. **Calendar cessation between 1 and 5 April 2027.** What are the deadlines for the final short-period update and the cessation notice, and does one follow by interpretation (for example regs 7(1)(b), 13(2)(b), 14)? *Now:* professional review (`CALENDAR_CESSATION_YEAR_BOUNDARY`), null deadlines, all four 2026/27 updates visible.
3. **Per-source or per-person calendar election.** The guidance selects calendar periods "for each source of income"; reg 11(1) frames the election as made by the relevant person for a tax year. Which governs, and do mixed elections need an input or a review stop? *Now:* one person-level `reporting.updatePeriod` applies to every source; a mix cannot be expressed or stopped; limits added.
4. **The 1–5 April 2026 stub for a calendar election.** Reg 5(3) moves the digital start date to "1st April in the tax year Y-1" when a calendar quarters election is made for the tax year Y, and reg 7(2)(b) says the digital obligation tax years then "begin with the following tax year". What records and updates does the 1–5 April 2026 stub require under reg 13(2)(b) and reg 15? *Now:* for a calendar election, `keep-digital-records` and every quarter's cumulative start is 2026-04-01.
5. **"Have submitted" or "required to deliver".** Does reg 5's duty-to-deliver test override the before-you-start wording "have submitted a tax return", so that a required but unsubmitted 2024/25 return still brings the person in? *Now:* professional review (`REQUIRED_RETURN_NOT_SUBMITTED`).
6. **Special income figures.** Should a jointly owned property share notified after expenses, and VAT included in business income by a VAT-registered business using the cash basis, be treated as "special rules" that the caller must flag? *Now:* supplied gross figures are summed as given; the special-rules flag leads to professional review; the boundary names these cases.
7. **Residence rule for later rows.** Does the page's residence rule, written for 2024/25, carry over to the 2025/26 and 2026/27 rows? Does excluding non-UK residents' foreign property follow from Schedule A1 TMA 1970 (not reviewed)? *Now:* the same rule is applied to all three rows; only the 2024/25 row decides 2026/27.
8. **Lloyd's (SA103L).** It appears under both the permanent and the beyond-April-2027 headings. Which duration applies? *Now:* professional review.
9. **Incapable with a legal representative.** Listed as permanent unless circumstances change. Could it be classified as exempt (reg 32, including any activity-level effect), which would be a new positive rule, or should the review stop stay? *Now:* professional review.
10. **SA104 partner route.** Should the `otherExemptionApplication` prompt name the SA104 partner averaging-relief application route? (A product or qualified-review decision.) *Now:* the prompt names applications based on a claim or page expected on a later return.
11. **Who counts as "confirmed exempt".** The circumstances-change statement applies "If HMRC has confirmed you're exempt". The cited exemptions page states generally that "If you are exempt, you will not have to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax but you must continue to report your income and gains in a Self Assessment tax return as normal", so only the circumstances-change wording is open. *Now:* exempt answers cite the exemptions and Self Assessment sources for the continuing return.
12. **Records after a final cessation.** How long must digital records be kept? The circumstances-change software example says "until at least 31 January 2033", and reg 15(3)(b)(ii) allows recording "no later than the date on which a return for that tax year is delivered, or required to be delivered". Should `keep-digital-records` extend beyond the cessation date? *Now:* `periodEnd` is the cessation date; the title says records must still be stored; no retention date.
13. **Statutory basis for the 2026/27 easement.** Should one be admitted alongside the guidance, for example SI 2026/370 (penalties appointed day for digitally obligated persons), which was seen in a title search but not read? *Now:* the 2026/27 position rests only on the penalties guidance.
14. **Balancing payments and payments on account.** Should the 31 January payment be described as a balancing payment (which would need, for example, SALF304 admitted), and should payments on account be modelled for this cohort? Context: the penalties page says "Late payment penalties do not apply to any payments on account that you may need to make". For 2026/27 the first payment on account would fall on 31 January 2027, before the mapped obligation. *Now:* one payment obligation; payments on account named as not mapped; no date added.
15. **Later notices to file.** How should SALF202's compressed notice-to-file exception be read against TMA 1970 s8(1F)/(1G), which distinguish notices given by 31 October from later ones? *Now:* later-notice exceptions are not computed; `doesNotProve` covers personalised deadlines.
16. **New SALF sections.** Do SALF900–SALF1600, the MTD sections of the manual (not reviewed), change filing or payment timing for in-scope or exempt individuals? *Now:* not admitted.
17. **Non-individual deadlines.** Do the paper-only deadlines for pension-scheme trustees and non-resident companies, and the partnership-with-company-partner deadlines, fall outside the individual sole-trader/landlord cohort? *Now:* `taxpayerTypes` is individual sole trader or landlord.

Optional, owner's choice rather than a narrowing: SALF202 is an internal manual section, and `TaxSourceKind` already has `hmrc-manual`; its kind stays `hmrc-guidance` for now. The capability registry's scope text for `uk.mtd-income-tax.readiness` does not yet list payments on account or mixed update periods among its exclusions.
